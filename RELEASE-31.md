# Warehouse Tycoon DOTv31 — Staff welfare and the round checklist

- **Invest → Welfare:** basic canteen £20,000, hot-food canteen £50,000, and staff lounge £125,000 (hot food, pool table and games console). All can be bought outright. Reusing the basic canteen credits £15,000 against a higher tier; reusing the hot-food canteen credits £40,000. Basic → Hot is £35,000, Hot → Lounge is £85,000. There is no required tier progression. The credit is not the full cost of the old build, so buying the eventual tier outright is cheaper overall.
- **Workforce mood:** the site gets one immediate boost at installation. An upgrade awards only the additional boost. Better facilities soften a fall in morale rather than erasing the effect of a poor manager. No ongoing bonus is added each month, and no absenteeism is introduced.
- **Home:** the current tier appears in a clickable cutaway beside the office. The room differs by tier, including hot kitchen and lounge equipment. Home's fixed camera, perimeter and warehouses without welfare are unchanged.
- **Complete month:** six pre-round checks appear only in the confirmation: bids, key renewals, resource plan, forecast coverage, investment and cash buffer. Tick for ready/action recorded, question mark for something to review, blank where no action is recorded. A live renewal representing at least 20% of forecast booked revenue is called out while a bid can still be placed. The checks are advisory; they do not prevent completing the month.
- The new save slot imports v30 (or the latest supported earlier save) and retains the original for rollback.
