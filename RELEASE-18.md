# DOTv18: trading, property and finance

- Storage is quoted per occupied pallet per turn; receipt, handling and despatch (RH&D) is quoted per billable movement. Receipts and despatches are separate, not extra charges for transfers within a warehouse. Collection remains in the same turn. Existing ordinary bid defaults and opening floor capacity stay unchanged.
- Spot work is occasional (34% offer chance per active region per turn), 50–250 pallets, one turn, one receipt and one despatch per pallet, no renewal. Separate visible SPOT badge, 1.65× market benchmark and blank-bid default; bids still compete on price and service. Capacity checks look at the highest overlapping booking in the actual term, so consecutive contracts can use the same slots.
- Ambient property overhead is £12/sq ft/year in the Midlands (£25,000 per 25,000 sq ft section per turn); tested regional starting assumptions are £10.50 North and £18 South East. No extra area discount. The rival model stays on its prior separate cost curve. These are scenario inputs, not market averages.
- A player extension costs £1m, takes six turns and counts as owned collateral only when complete. Existing projects retain their recorded capex and countdown; acquired companies do not turn their starter leases into assets. Prior player extensions without ownership metadata are inferred from non-starter sections. No private client example or tariff is included.
- New and migrated loans charge 8% nominal per year, with fixed payments clearing the balance in 36 turns. Prepaying principal can shorten the term. The credit limit is the lower of asset/business-value security headroom and six turns of demonstrated debt service cover, net of existing obligations. Asset advance is 55%, business-value ceiling 75% and minimum debt service cover 1.35×. Spot revenue does not inflate credit. No special opening loan was added; no debt is forgiven on migration or acquisition. Rival finance keeps its previous rules.
- Checks: automated coverage for exact overhead, unchanged rival rent, construction/collateral timing, spot terms and billing, scoped capacity, disabled opening bank, loan draw/amortisation and old debt, plus the game regression suite.
