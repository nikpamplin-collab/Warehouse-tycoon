# Warehouse Tycoon DOTv30 — People

- **People → Recruitment:** compare a monthly shortlist for Operations, Health & safety and Stock control. Four candidates in a boom; five in most other markets, until roles are filled. Better applicants usually ask for more, while downturns improve the candidate mix and reduce salary asks. Portraits and odd hobbies are fictional and do not affect performance. Operations candidates show experience, people management, safety focus and stock diligence. Specialists show experience and competency.
- **Site managers:** the Ops Office info keys describe effects in plain language. Hire in Recruitment, release only in Your team. All three roles can have multiple employees. Hiring adds to the team and counts their salary from the current month; it never dismisses an incumbent. Existing managers and legacy fixed salaries carry over; an experienced legacy appointment is not re-rated retrospectively.
- **Workforce mood:** a site-level face and direction. People management shifts it gradually, moving both manual and automated productivity. Replacing a manager does not reset mood. Safety and stock skills affect distinct incident risks; specialist roles are stronger and also reduce incident severity. In larger teams, average quality and additional coverage both matter, with diminishing returns. Exact modifiers stay out of gameplay copy. Fire protection remains a separate physical control. No absenteeism.
- **People → Your team:** view each manager's completed months, incident counts and worst severity while in post. Ops productivity shown only when there are enough similar months (same crew, kit and setup, and similar workload); an observation is not a causal attribution. Pre-upgrade observations are not invented.
- **Ops Office:** retains the v29 layout and uses actual shift-limited pallet-move capacity, with a one-line warning when an imbalance causes a shortage.
- The separate DOTv30 save imports the existing v29 save without overwriting it. No ZIP exchange is needed. A rollback to v29 uses that original save.
